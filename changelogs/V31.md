# V31

- Image generation now samples the downloaded Stable Diffusion checkpoint with a real on-device diffusion engine. Prompts such as "a white table" produce a picture of the subject instead of an abstract pattern of random colours.
- Video generation now draws its frames with an SD 1.5 checkpoint and an AnimateDiff motion module. The clips are much smaller to download than a full LTX video stack and follow the description with real motion.
- Clips get a narration track from the on-device text-to-speech model. The video weights are released before the voice loads, so the two never compete for memory. If no voice has been downloaded, the clip is saved without sound instead of failing.
- The built in procedural colour renderer has been removed. Repeated prompts produce repeatable results, and an explicit seed can still be supplied for a variation.
