# V26

## Added

- Added on-device video generation. The assistant can now turn a written description into a short clip and show it right in the conversation, where it can be played with sound.
- Added three video models that generate the picture and a synchronized soundtrack in the same pass: LTX-2.5 Distilled, LTX-2.5 22B and LTX-2 19B. All three use their smallest widely available Q2_K weights, and the distilled one is picked first because it needs the fewest sampling steps.
- Clips are saved with the chat as standard MP4 files with H.264 video and AAC audio, so they keep their sound after an update and work offline.

## Changed

- The Video tab of the Model Hub now lists the available models instead of saying that video generation is still in progress.
