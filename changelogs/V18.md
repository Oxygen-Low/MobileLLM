# V18

## Added

- You can now switch the voice that reads replies aloud. The Model Hub lists nineteen Piper voices in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Russian and Portuguese, each one downloaded on demand like the other speech models.
- Added a preview button that reads a short sample with the selected voice so you can hear it before you make it the voice of the app. The preview works even while Text to Speech is turned off and releases the voice again afterwards.
- Every voice can be fetched without switching to it, so you can download a few and listen to them side by side.

## Changed

- The voices now share a single copy of the phoneme data, which saves about 17 MB of storage for every voice you add.
